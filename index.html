About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Networking Group Aging, Department of Ergonomics, at the Leibniz Research Centre for Working Environment and Human Factors (IfADo), TU Dortmund University, Germany. My neuroscience research focuses on ADHD, attention, and cognitive function. I use behavioral, pharmacological, molecular, and proteomic approaches, and I am also interested in how metabolic and dietary factors affect brain function and neurotransmitter systems.

I hold a Ph.D. and an M.Sc. in Pharmacy (major in Pharmacology) from Sahmyook University, South Korea, as well as an M.A. in Nursing (Clinical Supervision) from the University of San Carlos and a B.S. in Nursing from Colegio de Sta. Lourdes of Leyte Foundation, Inc., Philippines. Before joining IfADo in June 2022, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Jeonbuk National University, South Korea (September 2021–May 2022).

Alongside neuroscience, I work in science, technology, and innovation policy research in the Philippines. I am an MRDM candidate at the University of the Philippines Open University (UPOU); I have successfully defended my thesis, and formal graduation is scheduled for December 2026. I was previously a member of the ARISE Research Group under UPOU's Policy Innovation and Learning Laboratory for Sustainability (PILLS) and now serve as a co-mentor in its Ecosystems Group, focusing on the National Innovation Ecosystem. ARISE Ecosystems Group.

Research

Behavioral and cognitive neuroscience

ADHD, attention, memory, behavioral phenotyping, aging-related cognitive changes, and preclinical models of ADHD, autism, addiction, and depression.

Neuropharmacology and addiction research

Pharmacological evaluation of psychoactive and novel psychoactive compounds, neurotransmitter systems, drug reward and reinforcement, abuse liability, and potential psychiatric treatment applications.

Molecular and systems neuroscience

Neurotransmitter signalling, molecular mechanisms, proteomics, EEG, and the effects of metabolic and dietary factors on brain function and behavior.

Science, technology, and innovation policy

National innovation ecosystems, Philippine STI policy, research assessment, research management, and multisectoral collaboration.
