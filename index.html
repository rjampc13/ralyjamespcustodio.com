About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Networking Group Aging, Department of Ergonomics, at the Leibniz Research Centre for Working Environment and Human Factors (IfADo), TU Dortmund University, Germany. My research focuses on ADHD, cognition, and aging-related changes in brain function. I use behavioral, pharmacological, and molecular approaches to study attention and cognitive function, including the effects of metabolic and dietary factors on the brain and neurotransmitter systems.

I hold a Ph.D. and an M.Sc. in Pharmacy (major in Pharmacology) from Sahmyook University, South Korea, as well as an M.A. in Nursing (Clinical Supervision) from the University of San Carlos and a B.S. in Nursing from Colegio de Sta. Lourdes of Leyte Foundation, Inc., Philippines.

Beyond neuroscience, I am interested in science, technology, and innovation policy in the Philippines, particularly innovation ecosystems, research management, and multisectoral collaboration.

Research

Behavioral and cognitive neuroscience

ADHD, attention, cognition, aging-related changes, and behavioral phenotyping in preclinical models of ADHD, addiction, autism, and depression.

Neuropharmacology and molecular neuroscience

Pharmacological mechanisms, neurotransmitter systems, and the effects of metabolic and dietary factors on brain function, using behavioral, neurophysiological, molecular, and proteomic approaches.

Science, technology, and innovation policy

Philippine innovation ecosystems, research management and assessment, and multisectoral collaboration.
