About

My research examines the neurobiological and behavioral mechanisms underlying attention, cognition, and psychiatric-relevant phenotypes. Through preclinical behavioral neuroscience and neuropharmacology, I investigate ADHD and neurodevelopment, drug reward and addiction vulnerability, sleep and circadian regulation, depression-related behaviors and antidepressant effects, and the behavioral and neurochemical actions of psychoactive compounds. My work integrates behavioral, pharmacological, molecular, gene-expression, and proteomic approaches. In parallel, I contribute to research on science, technology, and innovation policy, national innovation ecosystems, and research assessment.

Research

Behavioral and cognitive neuroscience

ADHD, attention, memory, behavioral phenotyping, aging-related cognitive changes, and preclinical models of ADHD, autism, addiction, and depression.

Neuropharmacology and addiction research

Pharmacological evaluation of psychoactive and novel psychoactive compounds, neurotransmitter systems, drug reward and reinforcement, abuse liability, and potential psychiatric treatment applications.

Molecular and systems neuroscience

Neurotransmitter signalling, molecular mechanisms, proteomics, EEG, and the effects of metabolic and dietary factors on brain function and behavior.

Science, technology, and innovation policy

National innovation ecosystems, Philippine STI policy, research assessment, research management, and multisectoral collaboration.

Research portfolio

Selected Research Studies and Ongoing Research

Selected completed, published, and ongoing studies across experimental neuroscience, neuropharmacology, and science, technology, and innovation policy. These research activities reflect both established lines of investigation and studies currently in progress; their inclusion does not imply external project funding.

ADHD · Preclinical model

THRSP-overexpressing mouse model of inattentive ADHD

Investigating attention and memory phenotypes, thyroid hormone and dopaminergic mechanisms, and molecular signatures using behavioral testing, proteomics, and pharmacological approaches. Related work examines potential environmental and exercise-based interventions.

ADHD · Longitudinal research

Longitudinal evaluation of ADHD across development

A research programme using DAT Val559 knock-in mice and wild-type controls to characterize developmental trajectories in ADHD-relevant behavior and cognition, integrating longitudinal behavioral phenotyping and EEG-based measures.

Addiction · Neuropharmacology

Neurobehavioral mechanisms of drug reward and abuse liability

Preclinical investigation of psychoactive and novel psychoactive substances, including stimulant, dissociative, cannabinoid, and other compound classes, with emphasis on reward, reinforcement, behavioral effects, and dopaminergic and circadian mechanisms.

Sleep · Depression

Pharmacological modulation of sleep and stress-related behavior

Research on sleep-promoting compounds and receptor mechanisms, alongside preclinical studies of antidepressant-like effects and neuropharmacological pathways in stress-related models.

STI policy · Philippines

Philippine national innovation ecosystem

Evidence synthesis on multisectoral participation and innovation-system fragmentation using the Quadruple Helix framework, alongside analysis of science, technology, and innovation policy development and implementation.

Research assessment · Higher education

Multidimensional research assessment and scholarly communication

Research on responsible and context-sensitive assessment of research performance in Philippine higher education, including the limitations of database-centric evaluation and the role of broader assessment dimensions.
